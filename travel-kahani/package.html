Route

Kochi Munnar Alleppey

Kochi → Munnar → Alleppey → Kochi, by private car.

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Arrive Kochi

    Fort Kochi walk, St Francis Church and the Chinese fishing nets.

  2. Day 2

    Drive to Munnar

    Waterfalls on the way up into the tea hills.

  3. Day 3

    Munnar sightseeing

    Eravikulam National Park, the tea museum and Mattupetty Dam.

  4. Day 4

    Alleppey houseboat

    Cruise the backwaters and stay overnight on board.

  5. Day 5

    Back to Kochi

    Drive back to Kochi and depart.
