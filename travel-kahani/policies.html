1

Booking

Travel Kahani is a tour planning business based in Hyderabad, Telangana. All enquiries are by WhatsApp or phone; nothing is booked or paid on this website.

A booking is confirmed only after we share a written quote and itinerary, you accept it, and we receive the advance.

Prices shown on the site are indicative; your final quote depends on dates, hotels, group size and availability.

2

Payments

An advance of 30% of the trip cost confirms your booking; the balance is due 15 days before departure. Bookings made within 15 days of travel are paid in full.

We accept payment only by bank transfer or UPI to the Travel Kahani business account, and send a receipt for every payment.

Please note

We never ask for cash or payment to a personal account.

Applicable taxes are shown on your quote and invoice.

3

Cancellations and refunds

When you cancel, counted before departure
You cancelWhat you get back
30 days or moreFull refund, minus non-refundable costs already paid to hotels, trains or airlines
15 to 29 days50% of the trip cost refunded
7 to 14 days25% refunded
Less than 7 days, or no-showNo refund

Refunds go to the original payment account within 10 working days of approval.

Flight, train and some hotel bookings follow their own rules, which we share with your quote.

If we cancel a trip, you get a full refund or a free date change.

4

Changes

We'll try to accommodate date, hotel or itinerary changes, subject to availability; any price difference applies.

Weather, strikes, road closures or other events beyond our control may change the itinerary; we'll offer the closest alternative.

5

Travel documents and safety

  • Carry a valid government photo ID for every traveller, and a passport for international trips.
  • Some areas need permits, for example Inner Line Permits in parts of the Northeast; we guide you on these.
  • Travel insurance is recommended for every trip.
  • Please follow local guidelines, dress codes at places of worship, and your guide's safety advice.

6

Our responsibility

We plan your trip with trusted hotels, drivers and guides, but they are independent providers. We are not liable for losses caused by their services, natural events, or travellers' own actions, though we will always help you sort out problems on the trip.

7

Privacy

We collect only what we need to plan your trip: names, contact details, ID details for bookings, and travel preferences.

We share them only with the hotels, transport and guides on your trip. We never sell your data.

Ask us anytime to see or delete your details.
